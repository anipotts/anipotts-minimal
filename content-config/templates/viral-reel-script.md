## Production Notes

- **Film on:** Phone (portrait mode, 9:16)
- **Edit in:** Meta app (Instagram built-in editor) or CapCut
- **Lighting:** Natural light or ring light, face visible
- **Audio:** Original audio (your voice), add trending sound underneath if relevant
- **Text:** Use bold, readable text overlays for key points
- **Transitions:** Simple cuts, no fancy transitions needed
- **Speed:** Fast cuts between face and screen. No dead air.
- **Total duration:** UNDER 30 seconds. Shorter = better for this format.
