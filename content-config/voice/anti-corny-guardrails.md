# Anti-Corny Guardrails

> If it makes you cringe reading it back, it'll make them cringe too. Kill it.

## The 5 Hard NOs

### 1. NO Fake Vulnerability
**What it looks like:**
- "I used to be so lost until I discovered..."
- "I'll be honest, I was scared to share this..."
- "This might be controversial but..." (then says something everyone agrees with)
- "I wasn't sure I should post this..." (has been workshopping it for 3 days)

**What to do instead:**
- Share real struggles with specifics: "Spent 4 hours debugging a CORS error that turned out to be a missing trailing slash"
- Be matter-of-fact: "This didn't work the first time. Here's what I changed."
- Skip the preamble, just share the thing

---

### 2. NO Engagement Farming
**What it looks like:**
- "Comment '🔥' if you agree!"
- "Tag someone who needs to see this"
- "Share this with a friend who..."
- "Like if you've ever..."
- "Repost for reach 🙏"
- Asking questions you don't actually care about the answer to

**What to do instead:**
- Ask genuine questions: "Has anyone tried X? Curious what your setup looks like"
- End with a real CTA: "If you try this, lmk what you build"
- Let the content earn engagement — if the hook is good, they'll share it

---

### 3. NO Guru Energy
**What it looks like:**
- "Here's my framework for success..."
- "The 7 habits of highly effective..."
- "Most people don't know this secret..."
- "I've cracked the code on..."
- "Let me teach you how to 10x your..."
- Positioning yourself as having all the answers
- Speaking in absolutes about complex topics

**What to do instead:**
- "Here's what worked for me" (not "here's what works")
- "I found this useful" (not "you need this")
- Share discoveries, not declarations
- Include what you're still figuring out
- "This is one approach — there are others"

---

### 4. NO Hype Without Receipts
**What it looks like:**
- "This tool is INSANE 🤯"
- "Game-changer!!" with no explanation of what changed
- "This will revolutionize how you..."
- "You NEED to try this" with no proof it works
- Superlatives without specifics

**What to do instead:**
- Show the output: "Here's what Claude Code built in 45 seconds" + screenshot/gist
- Include numbers: "Cut my deploy time from 12 minutes to 90 seconds"
- Link to proof: gist, repo, screen recording, before/after
- Let the result speak: "I asked Claude Code to refactor this file. Here's the diff."

---

### 5. NO Recycled Platitudes
**What it looks like:**
- "The best time to start was yesterday, the second best is today"
- "You miss 100% of the shots you don't take"
- "Work smarter, not harder"
- "It's not about the destination, it's about the journey"
- "Consistency is key" (with nothing else)
- Any phrase that could go on a motivational poster

**What to do instead:**
- Make it specific to your experience: "I posted every day for 3 weeks and my engagement tripled — but only because I stopped posting generic tips and started showing my actual screen"
- If you catch yourself writing a platitude, delete it and write what you ACTUALLY mean
- The specific version is always better than the general one

---

## QA Checklist (Run Before Every Post)

Before publishing any atom, check ALL five:

- [ ] **No fake vulnerability** — Am I being genuinely honest, or performing honesty?
- [ ] **No engagement farming** — Would the CTA still make sense if zero people responded?
- [ ] **No guru energy** — Am I sharing what I found, or telling people what to do?
- [ ] **No hype without receipts** — Is there a proof artifact (gist/repo/screenshot) linked or described?
- [ ] **No recycled platitudes** — Could I swap in any other creator's name and it still works? If yes, rewrite.

### The Ultimate Test

Read the post out loud in your normal speaking voice. If any sentence makes you pause or change your inflection to sound more "content creator-y," that sentence needs to go.

---

## Replacement Patterns

| Instead of... | Try... |
|---|---|
| "Game-changer" | Describe what specifically changed |
| "You need to try this" | "Here's what happened when I tried this" |
| "Most people don't know..." | Just share the thing directly |
| "Let me be real with you..." | Just be real without announcing it |
| "I'm so grateful for this community" | Show the gratitude through useful content |
| "Drop a 🔥 if..." | Ask a real question or say nothing |
| "This one simple trick..." | Describe the technique and show results |
| "Unlock your potential" | Delete this and start over |

---

## When In Doubt

Ask yourself: **"Would I say this to a friend sitting next to me?"**

If no → rewrite it.
If yes → ship it.
