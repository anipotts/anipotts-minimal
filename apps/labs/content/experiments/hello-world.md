This page is the human side of [labs.anipotts.com](https://labs.anipotts.com). The bot side lives at [`anipotts/labs`](https://github.com/anipotts/labs) and gets pulled in at build time.

## Why two repos

The split exists so the bot/human contract holds. `anipotts/labs` only ever changes when an autonomous run produced something a person would want to read in retrospect. Putting human-authored prose alongside it would defeat the point: the commit log on that repo is supposed to be the changelog of what the framework actually did.

Experiments live here, in the monorepo. They share the same lint and deploy pipeline as the rest of the site, and bots stay isolated.

## What you'll find here

- **Weekly digests.** Auto-generated by `~/Infra/bin/digest`, committed to the bot repo via `pro-commit`.
- **Experiments.** Tidied work I'm comfortable showing in public: code spikes, infra writeups, postmortems on dead-ends, half-finished prototypes that taught me something worth keeping.

If you came here looking for finished work, that's at [anipotts.com](https://anipotts.com). This is the sketchbook.
